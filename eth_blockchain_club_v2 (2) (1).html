Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

ETH Blockchain Club is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Swiss revDSG and EU GDPR where applicable.

  • Identity & contact data when you apply or register

  • Event registration and attendance records
  • Newsletter email and subscription preferences

How We Use It

  • Processing membership and event registrations
  • Sending club newsletters (with consent)
  • Communicating about open positions
  • Improving our website and services

Your Rights

Code of Ethics

Adopted: September 2024

Our Code of Ethics defines the values guiding every member, committee, and partnership decision.

Integrity & Honesty

We are truthful in all communications. We acknowledge mistakes and correct them promptly.

Inclusivity & Respect

  • Open to all regardless of background or experience
  • Discrimination and harassment lead to immediate removal
  • We actively work to make blockchain more accessible

Responsible Innovation

We encourage members to consider the ethical implications of what they build — energy use, financial risk, and potential for misuse.

Conflict of Interest

Members must disclose personal financial interests in projects or organisations discussed in club activities.

Reporting Violations

Report confidentially to ethics@ethblockchain.ch. All reports are reviewed by the board with protections for the reporter.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Cookies We Use

  • Essential — session management and security. Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference — remember your settings. Active 12 months.
  • Analytics — anonymised usage data. Privacy-first, no cross-site tracking.

We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. No ad networks, no retargeting.

Your Choices

Control cookies via your browser settings. Contact privacy@ethblockchain.ch to opt out of analytics.
